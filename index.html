How to Play

  • Place your ships on your board (left) by clicking cells
  • Use the "Rotate Ship" button or press 'R' to change orientation
  • Click on the enemy board (right) to attack
  • Red = Hit, Gray = Miss
  • Sink all 5 enemy ships to win!
  • Ships: Carrier(5), Battleship(4), Cruiser(3), Submarine(3), Destroyer(2)
